# First Version Inputs

The first version should know enough to make meaningful guesses, but not so much that it becomes invasive or impossible to build.

## 1. Location context

- current location
- movement through locations over time
- rough place type if it can be inferred cheaply
- saved meaningful places
- revisited places

Why:
- needed for meaningful pause, leaving a place, returning, discovery, and belonging

## 2. Movement context

- whether you are moving or stationary
- how long you have been stationary
- whether movement is unusual for that time or day
- whether you are lingering longer than normal

Why:
- needed to detect pauses, transitions, and breaks in rhythm

## 3. Time context

- time of day
- day of week
- likely sleep window
- recurring weekly patterns

Why:
- needed for respecting boundaries and understanding rhythm

## 4. Memory context

- short notes about meaningful recent events
- previous outreach and whether it felt right
- places that became meaningful over time
- things you explicitly said matter
- things you explicitly said do not matter

Why:
- needed so it becomes more you-shaped over time

## 5. Manual reflection input

- a way for you to tell it:
  - "this mattered"
  - "this didn't"
  - "don't bring this up"
  - "this place means something"
  - "this week feels heavy / beautiful / uncertain"

Why:
- because not everything meaningful can be inferred

## 6. Communication response context

- whether you answered
- whether you declined
- whether the message or call felt welcome
- whether it reached out too much

Why:
- needed so it learns timing and trust

## Optional later, not first

- weather
- public events
- local safety alerts
- calendar
- wearable or biometric signals
- richer map semantics beyond cheap local inference

Why these are later:
- they may add value, but they are not necessary to prove the core concept
- some may add cost, complexity, or intrusiveness too early
- the first version should stay focused on rhythm, place, memory, reflection, and trust
